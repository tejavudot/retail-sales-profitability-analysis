# retail-sales-profitability-analysis
Retail sales and profitability analysis using PostgreSQL and Excel
Purpose: Compare retail sales and profitability, then investigate losses in the Tables subcategory.
Tools: PostgreSQL and Excel.
Findings so far: Tables’ margin was −8.10%; 112 of 170 products (65.88%) were loss-making; the worst 10 contributed 29.69% of losses among loss-making products.
Limitations: Causes of the losses haven’t been established, and the Discount field’s meaning remains unverified.
Status: In progress; dashboard and final report still to come.
